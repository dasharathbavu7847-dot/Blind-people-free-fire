Movement

Turn Left: Rotate Left.

Weapon Gestures

Single Tap: Select or activate control.

Double Tap: Fire Gun.

Swipe Left: Change to Previous Gun.

Swipe Right: Change to Next Gun.

Swipe Up: Reload Magazine.

Swipe Down: Detect Enemy.

Two Finger Swipe Up: Use Medkit.
